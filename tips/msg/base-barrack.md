## Base Barrack
Have you ever merged multiple troops for Arena and later discovered empty slots in your HH/IW formations? If so, you are not alone!

Below is a suggested barrack for maintaining good scores in HH and IW. When upgrading a troop for Arena, keep in mind of what you need for HH/IW so your decision doesn't come back to bite you.

- `15` **Frost Archers** (8 L9+, 7 L8)
- `12` Taurus Witcher (12 L9+)
- `19` Slingshot Barbarian (19 L7+)
- `22` **Straw Kids** (12 L9+, 10 L6+)
- `14` Magic Apprentice (12 L9+, 2 L8)
- ` 8` Harbinger of Fire (8 L9+)
- `13` **Voodoo Dolls** (13 L7+)
- ` 6` **Nun** (6 L9+, 2 L8)
- ` 4` Heal Spirit (4 L9+)
- ` 5` Pilgrims (3 L9+, 2 L8)
- ` 4` Brawlers (4 L9+)
- ` 9` **Peltasts** (4 L9+, 5 L8)
- ` 5` Ballista
- ` 6` Dwarf Artillerymen
- `11` **Candle Warrior** (1 L10, 2 L9, 2 L8,...)
- `12` **Necromancer** (12 or 14)
- ` 7` **Pumpkin Guard** (1 L9+, 6 L7+)
- `10` Mummy (10 L7+)
- ` 5` Mechanical Chaser (5 L9+)
- ` 4` Royal Musket Guards (6 L9+)
- `46` Goblin Tech (10 L9+, 36 L8)
- `13` Magnetic Pioneer (4 L9+, 9 L8)
- ` 2` Yasha (2 L8)
- ` 4` Meteor Golem (4 L8)
- ` 2` Undead Soldier (2 L9)
- ` 4` Undead Warrior (2 L9)
- ` 6` Shadow Ninja (6 L9)
- ` 2` Tauntman (2 L8)
- `11` Flash Angels (6 L9+, 5 L8)
- ` 9` War Drum Gorilla (9 L8)
- ` 4` Bloodthirsty Devil (2 L9+, 2 L8)
- ` 3` Aegis Purifier (2 L9+, 1 L8)

---

You need up to **45 archer units** for HH. Taurus Witcher change target when activating skill and are useful for taking out small spiders in Spider Queen HH. Slingshot Barbarian should be kept at L7 at first so you have more freedom in making L9+ FA and TW

---

As of November 2026, you need up to **22 Straw Kids** for IW. Harbinger of Fire and Magic Apprentice both have AoE (area-of-effect) damage and can work as fillers for SK in Infinity War. Upgrade these to L9+ so you have more freedom in making L9+ SK.

---

In Arena, Voodoo Dolls' *curse skill can fail if the enemy target is at a higher level*. To use VD in Arena, you need them at max level and in high quantity. In the beginning, it's better to keep VD at lower levels (L7+) for Honor Hunting.

---

Nun are critical in Cerberus HH. Cerberus has damage reflection skill, and the more damage your archer units deal, the more Nun you need. If you use Jinn in Cerberus, 6 Nun should be sufficient. If you use Sylvax in Cerberus, you may need 8 Nun in Cerberus. Also, if you use Copernicus in IW, you may need up to 8 Nun.

---

Some IW maps require Peltasts in large quantity. If you don't want to make many L9+ Peltasts specifically for IW, you can use Ballista and Dwarf Artillerymen as fillers in IW. Ballista also work as fillers in Cyclops.

---

Candle Warrior require careful barrack management. In some HH and IW campaigns, you will get better results with CW at various levels (for example, L9 + L8 + L6). What levels you need is very situational, and you have to test it out yourself. It is best to keep at least 2 CW at each level.

---

Necromancer are essential in Spider Queen and Ceberus. In phase 3 of Spider Queen, you need 12 Necromancer if you use Jinn, and you may need 14 Necromancer if you use Sylvax.

---

Pumpkin Guard can reduce HH boss's attack and help increase survivability of your troops. You need 1 PG for Leviathan. If you just started the game and use a melee-based formation in HH, you need 1 PG.

In Ice Dragon, you need around 17 of PG and Mummy combined. The exact number is situational and you need to test it out yourself. If you use them at level 8 or lower, a L8 PG is equivalent to a L7 Mummy, and a L7 PG is equivalent to a L6 Mummy.
